How to adopt and monitor

Your simple tasks (only takes 5 minutes)

1 Select a safe coastal site with a species of algae, seagrass or animal forest you can revisit.
2 Using iNaturalist application, upload your photos from fixed viewpoints.
3 In the notes field, type #adoptmf to tag your observation.
4 Visit the site once a month (idealy) to a reliable time series.
