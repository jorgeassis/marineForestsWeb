Data catalogue

Open-source data covering the global distribution and diversity of Marine Forests.

Literature and herbaria records
Fine-tuned dataset of marine forests compiled from literature and herbaria, with dereplicated occurrence records, taxonomically standardized, and considering important physiological and biogeographical traits.
iNaturalist records
Distribution records of marine forests compiled from iNaturalist.org, an online social network of people sharing biodiversity information to help each other learn about nature.

Genetic diversity data
Population genetic diversity and differentiation data of marine forests compiled from the available literature.
