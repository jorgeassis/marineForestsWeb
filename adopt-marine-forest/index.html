What you'll help discover

How to adopt and monitor

Your simple tasks

1 Select a safe coastal site you can revisit consistently.
2 Using iNaturalist project 'Adopt a marine forest', upload your photos from fixed viewpoints and take notes about the local conditions (e.g., anchoring, tidal height, etc.).
3 Visit the site regularly to build a reliable time series.
